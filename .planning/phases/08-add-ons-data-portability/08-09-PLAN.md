## RESEARCH.md Pitfall 5: AddonRegistry enable state not observed by SiderMenu

**Mitigation:** SiderMenu reads nav items from navConfig statically. To ensure disabled add-on pages don't appear: the navigation selectors or SiderMenu must filter nav items by `addonRegistry.isEnabled(id)`. Add a `subscribe` or `useEffect` in SiderMenu that listens to `chrome.storage.onChanged` for `np_addon_enabled` and re-filters visible add-on items. This is addressed in Task 1 of this plan by making the navConfig reading dynamic OR by having SiderMenu filter at render time.
