<objective>
Freeze the canonical workspace contract, build the request-correlated handoff protocol, and give both surfaces a validated open path.

Purpose: D-11 freezes the full §8.4 `WorkspaceState` shape in Phase 1 with later-phase fields inert, and D-13 defines the handoff as URL bootstrap identifiers plus a ready/transfer/acknowledgement handshake on `BroadcastBus` — while D-14 forbids persisting `WorkspaceState` at all. The prototype does the opposite on every count: it persists to `np_workspace_store`, publishes a fire-and-forget notice with no correlation, and lets a raw model selector write `selectedModel`. This plan replaces that behaviour with the contract later phases extend, and proves the protocol's hard cases — cold tab, duplicate request id, timeout, retry, ack gate, malformed URL, unsupported version, invalid surface, and draft-never-in-URL.
Output: a strict validated `WorkspaceState`, a projection-allowlisted handshake with named timeout/retry constants, a store with no persistence, and a router that dedupes, validates and gates its success claim on a validated acknowledgement.
</objective>
