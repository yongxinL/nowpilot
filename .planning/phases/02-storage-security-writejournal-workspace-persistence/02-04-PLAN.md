## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-2-04-01 | Tampering | replay of a foreign-workspace entry | high | mitigate | M.3 workspaceId scope gate applied in recoverWorkspaceJournal replay (D-07, WR-10) — a recovered write can never contaminate another workspaceId; asserted in unit case 5. |
| T-2-04-02 | Denial of Service | unknown-op entry bricking startup | high | mitigate | Unknown-op replay = skip + debugLog, never throw (D-07) — a forward-compat entry cannot crash recoverJournal; asserted in unit case 4. |
| T-2-04-03 | Tampering | non-idempotent replay duplicating writes | high | mitigate | Every apply() is an idempotent versioned upsert (pickActive snapshot by version); replay-once is asserted (unit case 3); runJournaled persists status at every boundary so a crash mid-write leaves an 'applying' entry, never a half-applied one. |
| T-2-04-04 | Information Disclosure | journal entries logged unredacted | medium | mitigate | journal debugLog calls route through debugLog's TraceRedactor (02-02); no raw prompt/secret ever lands in a journal entry or its step error fields (step errors are debugLog-strings only). |
| T-2-04-05 | Availability | rollback failure loop | medium | mitigate | Each rollback is individually wrapped; a rollback throw is logged as WRITE_JOURNAL_ROLLBACK_FAILED and does NOT mask the original error or abort the remaining rollbacks (O.11 verbatim). |
</threat_model>
