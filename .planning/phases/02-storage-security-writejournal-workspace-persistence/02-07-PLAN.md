| T-02-12 | Denial of Service | `ErrorStore` — unbounded error log growth | medium | mitigate | FIFO enforcement at 100 entries in `logError()`. Oldest errors are evicted when limit exceeded. |
| T-02-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo | high | mitigate | No new packages. |
